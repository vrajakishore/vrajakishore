## Philosophy & Approach

### Core Beliefs
1. **Implementation over ideology** — Practical solutions beat perfect theory
2. **Operations-first thinking** — Systems must be run and supported by humans
3. **Visible trade-offs** — Document constraints and decisions clearly
4. **Testable confidence** — Validate assumptions before scaling
5. **Continuous learning** — Technology changes; stay curious and adaptive

### Design Principles
- **Observable**: Monitoring and logging built in from day one
- **Reliable**: Graceful degradation, error handling, redundancy where it matters
- **Maintainable**: Clear code, documented patterns, operational runbooks
- **Secure**: Least privilege, encryption, audit trails
- **Cost-conscious**: Efficient resource usage without sacrificing quality
